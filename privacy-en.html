Privacy Policy

BokBulBall ("Service") values your privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other relevant legislation. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights regarding your data.

Data Controller

  • Name: Clapbin
  • Email: clapbin0178@gmail.com

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at the email address above.

1. Personal Data We Collect

  • Required: Device identifier (Device UUID), Nickname
  • Optional: Push notification token, notification language, device time zone, and the device’s country/region setting (if you allow board notifications or BokBulBall push — location data is not collected)
  • Automatically collected: IP address, Service usage records (post and comment history, report and sanction records, etc.), anonymous usage statistics (app version, country/region setting, counts of launches, games, and feature use — aggregated information that cannot identify individuals)

2. Purposes of Data Collection

  • User identification and prevention of confusion between community users
  • Prevention of abuse (spam, malicious posts/comments, illegal activity) and enforcement of sanctions against unauthorized conduct
  • Management of daily posting and commenting limits for system stability
  • Processing inquiries and delivering service notifications
  • Sending notifications about comments, likes, and tags on your posts and comments (can be turned off at any time in Settings)
  • Sending BokBulBall push (service tips and event news) at your local time (can be turned off at any time in Settings)
  • Analyzing anonymous usage statistics to improve the Service
  • Cooperation with law enforcement authorities in accordance with applicable laws

3. Data Retention Period

  • Your personal data will be deleted without delay upon service termination or upon your request (account deletion, app removal, etc.).
  • Exception: To prevent abuse and circumvention of sanctions through voluntary withdrawal, device identifiers and sanction records of sanctioned users will be retained for 6 months from the date of sanction and then deleted.
  • Additionally, where required by applicable law, personal data may be retained for the period specified by such law.

4. Right to Refuse and Consequences

You have the right to refuse consent to the collection and use of personal data. As this consent is required for use of the community and bulletin board features, refusal will restrict your ability to create posts and comments. (However, read-only access to posts is available without consent.)

5. Third-Party Data Transfers

  • To automatically detect inappropriate content (profanity, violence, hate speech, etc.) in the community, the text content of posts and comments written by users is transmitted to the Content Moderation API provided by OpenAI, Inc. (United States).
  • Data transmitted: Post titles, post content, comment content (text only; no personal identifiers such as device IDs are transmitted)
  • Purpose: Automatic detection and filtering of inappropriate content
  • For information about OpenAI's data handling practices, please refer to the OpenAI Privacy Policy.
  • To deliver notifications, push notification tokens are processed through Google LLC (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple Inc. (Apple Push Notification service). Notification content may include the author's nickname and part of the post or comment. BokBulBall push is sent to recipient groups (topics) by language, time zone, and country, and we do not keep a list of individual recipients on our servers.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal)
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at clapbin0178@gmail.com or use the "Delete My Data" feature within the app settings.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be announced within the app. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
